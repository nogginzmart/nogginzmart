what is this?
Glad you asked!

After getting help from howsoonisnow's guide to help make my new homepage quizzes, I felt inspired by how they have a whole page dedicated to recording the results of their quizzes!

I figured it might be fun to do! Also to help me stay motivated with changing the quizzes semi often & perhaps making more engaging quizzes! I really love hearing other people's perspectives on things, so even if it's over something more complicated or just simple it's cool how different people can come to a different conclusion or preference based on a singular idea or event!

At the bottom of the left bar there's a history of older quiz summaries! Click on one of the dates if you wanna see the quiz & what the results were!

This is by no means scientific--I can barely get over 15 people to answer my quizzes over several months. It's just for fun! Unless you want to go off a sample size of 2 pennies--then by all means consider it gospel.

Anyways, have fun reading! see you around!
