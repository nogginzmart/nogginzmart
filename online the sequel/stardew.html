What Is 'Stardew Valley'?

According to Wikipedia,

"Stardew Valley is a 2016 arm life simulation video game developed by Eric "ConcernedApe" Barone. Players take the role of a character who inherits their deceased grandfather's dilapidated farm. The game is open-ended, with players managing the farm by clearing land, growing seasonal crops, and raising animals. Players can acquire skills in farming, foraging, fishing, mining, and combat. Social features include building relationships with townspeople, with the option to marry and have a child. A later update added multiplayer, allowing online play with others."

Furthermore, the Stardew Offical Wiki describes

"Stardew Valley is an open-ended country-life RPG! You’ve inherited your grandfather’s old farm plot in Stardew Valley. Armed with hand-me-down tools and a few coins, you set out to begin your new life. Can you learn to live off the land and turn these overgrown fields into a thriving home? It won’t be easy. Ever since Joja Corporation came to town, the old ways of life have all but disappeared. The community center, once the town’s most vibrant hub of activity, now lies in shambles. But the valley seems full of opportunity. With a little dedication, you might just be the one to restore Stardew Valley to greatness!"

Where do I Find Mods?

click to reveal!

Mods you can find all over the place, but my go to is Nexus Mods!

Nexus isn't perfect, but I find it useful as a go-to place for all things mods. The only other place I've gotten mods from is Github, but that can be a little harder to read if you're not used to the format of Github

On Nexus, there are lots of different factors when looking at a mod entry to know if/what you need to download that mod!

There are a couple of things to look at when you click onto a mod page

  • when was the mod last updated?
    if it hasn't been updated for latest updates, check if it has required mods, such as Content Patcher, that make last updates irrelevant
  • what mods are required for this mod to work?
    under "About This Mod", there should be several "drop-down" menus listing different things. The first one, for most mods, is "Requirements". Click here to check what a mod may require. The mod author USUALLY adds if the mod is actually required OR just recommended for an opitmal experience.
  • did the mod pass the virus scan?
    On Nexus, where is lists "last updated" "uploaded", the last one listed will be called "virus scan". Check to be sure it calls it safe. Though 9 times out of 10 you WILL be fine.
  • be sure you're downloading the latest, working mod ZIP
    Read over the mod entry, make sure that when you go to download you are picking the latest version of that mod. Otherwise it could just simply not work or mess something up in your game. Uploaded mods will have "Date Uploaded" on it, I usually check that to see if that is the latest version of that mod.
  • likewise, make sure the mod is compatible with the required mods you downloaded
    For mods such as Content Patcher or Fashion Sense, most mods that are compatible with those mods will have (CP) or (FS) at the beginning of their name. Be sure you have the latest, applicable mod that works with your required mods!

& that's pretty much it!

It isn't rocket science! (thankfully) Most of this stuff is lists on Nexus mods entries, so just reading over & being aware of what you're downloading is all you really need to do when picking a mod to download!

but are you unsure what to pick or start with? Well, as someone who has had 99+ mods on a file before, I've got plenty I've used to try out!

Mod Upkeep

click to reveal!

Stardew is modded! HURRAY!! You should be proud!!

But let's imagine that there's been an update to the game itself, some of your mods stop working as a result! OR you download a mod & it isn't working properly!
what do you do???

do no fear! I'm here to help!

As updates come out, patches can cause mods to stop working or, with modders updating their mods, could result in the older versions ceasing to work. This happens! Even if there aren't actual game patches/updates, mod authors will release updated patches to their mods! It's just a matter of routinely checking to make sure your mods are all running smoothly! But how does one fix this predicament?

Step 1

When opening the game, check out the window where Smapi is starting up. in RED there should be the mods that aren't working! in PURPLE, it can show what mods have updates SMAPI CAN DETECT!

write down on paper or take a screenshot of the mods not working or those that have detectable updates. Then go to your mod site of choice & search them up!

Step 2

BEFORE DOWNLOADING UPDATES, Go into your mod folder on your computer for THE GAME ITSELF & DELETE the old mods that HAVE AVAILABLE UPDATES

Duplicates of mods can break them. Make sure you keep your folder nice & clean of old versions of mods to keep your mods running smoothly!

Step 3

You've added your updated mods, kept your folder clean, & started the game. If you put them in correctly, these mods should be up & running again!
If not, you may have to wait for an update OR see if there's dependencies (required mods) for your broken mods

When the game gets updated, dependencies for mods may change. If there's added content, authors may decide to add more dependencies to keep added content in the same vibe or vein as other, preestablished content.

Step 4 (optional, but important!)

DELETE mods that are perpetually broken/no available fixes after patches to prevent any issues with your modded Stardew.

As the game gets older, some mod authors may move to different projects & choose to stop updating mods for Stardew. That is their right as the creators of the mods! Though as a result, they become unusable in newer patches/versions of the game.

Although it's sad, it's the inevitable reality for all mods! That's why mods like Content Patcher are great because they keep mods alive & working even if the author chooses to stop updating.

Though this shouldn't deter you from modding! We should enjoy things while they're available to us, & these authors put a lot of work into these mods that make this game have so many more hours of enjoyment. Plus, who knows, maybe other people will life the mod enough to release an updated, unofficial patch! I have seen people do that! Just make sure to keep on top of updates & keep everything running smoothly!

& for those, like me, that can go long periods without playing sometimes--

You need to triple check that your mods (if you haven't deleted them entirely after some time) are updated & working. Hell, you might need to even update Smapi. This happens! Just know that general upkeep will make THIS part a LOT less daunting. Especially if you're like me & have 5 billion mods.
