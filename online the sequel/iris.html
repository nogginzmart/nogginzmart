iris's page

learn more about my character, iris!

Iris is one of the side characters in my story, Dedicated To The Kidz I Fought. She is one of Erin's older sisters. Being a fraternal twin with Rae. She's not the most social person, but if you approach her she's kind & willing to help!

Although all three of the siblings are anti-social in their own ways, Iris is probably the most social & approachable. Though she struggles with understanding what people say sometimes--especially when there's so many other noises going on. Plus she's always sporting her beloved pair of headphones.

She is quite dedicated to her studies, enjoying school, but she would be lying if she didn't get bored if they don't move through content fast enough. Usually getting up to her variety of hobbies to pass the time. Of which lands her in trouble sometimes with her teachers.

Iris is always beside her best friend, Cecilia! The two always yapping about something nerdy or whatever captivates their attention at that time. The two sticking together & try & drown out the bullying from their classmates. Them being targetted for respective reasons...

what are they like?

  • Iris definitely tries her best when it comes to interacting with people, always trying to make the best impression. Though sometimes she won't anticipate someone talking to her, so someone can say something to her & she won't understand a word that you said. Usually frustrating people & makes her feel guilty for not giving her attention to you.
  • She has definitely given Thomas a scare or two when she's taken it upon herself to tinker with his computer. Usually updating components, cleaning, or general tinkering to understand it. He usually has to remind her to ask, but she never remembers when she gets the urge or plan to do so. He's usually the one buying the parts she's hankering for--he's just happy she has a hobby that keeps her always learning.
  • Furthermore, she's almost landed herself in hot water modding their gaming consoles (especially since they are NOT cheap & they're not just her's but those she shares with her siblings.) She usually has to explain what she's doing & swear it's nothing that's going to break it, but he encourages her to...just ask or let them know if she decides to do something to family devices.
  • When it comes to school, she sticks out like a sore thumb in her computer club meetings. Not to mention her going their on her free time to have a better space & available resources to work on her own personal computer. Though because of what she looks like, some of the boys in the club make it their mission to try & make her their girlfriend, MUCH to her dismay. Their advances &...more often than not, staring making her incredibly uncomfortable.
  • Although she loves theater, she doesn't actively perform on stage (she'll pass out like a fainting goat). So she deals with tech stuff--usually dealing with the music, lights, or effects. She doesn't plan to change that, but she is thoroughly satisfied seeing her friend Cecilia perform on stage.
  • Thomas has has to fight a lot for Iris when it comes to her schooling. When she was younger, she struggled a lot more with speech & stimuli, which made her stand out in a normal class setting. Them putting her in special education so she wouldn't disrupt the flow of a normal class.
  • Though she was thoroughly bored--the curriculum very much unchanging between the varying levels of disbility between the students. As a result, she would get in trouble for "rushing through work" or finding something else to do to occupy her time when she was done with work. Not to mention when she got overwhelmed by the breakdowns or disruptions of other students.
  • So after thorough chastizing & fighting with the staff dealing with her grade group, Iris was able to return to normal classes, though with safety nets to accomodate her specific needs. It didn't take her very long to catch back up, Thomas making sure she caught up to speed so she wouldn't feel discouraged or frustrated in class. He's her number 1 advocate, & she couldn't be more grateful.
  • Iris loves her siblings, much to both of their dismay. Iris makes fun of their edgy, broody nature--especially Erin--comparing them to Rae's level of angst. Though she's always there to listen if they had something on their mind--lingering if she notices them acting particularly off. Especially since they all overlap with certain issues, which makes her heartache.
