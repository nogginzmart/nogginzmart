learn more about my character, thomas!

Thomas is one of the main characters in my story, Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought. Thomas is preparing for the Fall 2008 school year to begin--working as a Junior regular & honors English teacher at the local school, Crested Butte Community School. He quite enjoys his job, so he's glad to get back into the swing of things.

Though he can't help but worry about Erin, his 2nd cousin that's in his care. They're incredibly withdrawn, & with their 7th grade year starting up, he hopes they can make this a good, new start to another school year.

He's thankful that, during this economically trying time, he's secure & entirely owns the home he lives in. Food being pretty easy to ration considering he's only feeding himself & Erin. Though his pseudo sister, Véronica, struggles incredibly with working as an independent mechanic with 3 mouthes to feed, not including herself. He does his best to pitch in to help her where he can, which he faces a lot of push back on her account, but the last thing he wants is for her to struggle even more than she already is.

Due to work, taking care of Erin, & personal projects--Thomas tends to neglect his social life. Sure, he goes out with Véronica on Saturdays to drink at local bars, but other than that he...really doesn't know anybody else in their small mountain town that he hasn't taught, taught their kids, or works with at the school.

Véronica wants him to live a little, which she's been desperately trying to get him to do for...almost the entire time they've known each other. He just worries he's just not as...interesting as Veronica makes him out to be.

what are they like?

  • Thomas is a quiet guy--cherishes his alone time, gravitates towards hobbies where he can do them alone, & does have much of a social life.
  • It doesn't mean he isn't friendly or kind, quite the contrary, though if you see him talking to someone it's usually his pseudo sister, Véronica, a parents of one of his students, one of or a former student of his, or a coworker. Of which eliminates quite a bit of people in their small, mountain town.
  • It also doesn't help he lives 30 minutes from town--all the way up the mountain. It's a beautiful place, having inherited it from his mother. He takes great pride in keeping it up & functioning. Though it definitely isolates him from the going on's in town.
  • Although he never planned on being a parent, he does his best when it comes to taking care of his 2nd cousin, Erin. Even after previous events that threw their lives into a whirlwind, he still does his best to prioritize their needs. Even if their parental/child relationship isn't the most conventional.
  • Yes, he is THAT old. Though the only thing about him that's really changed is his hair--having gone grey over the years. It's a running joke with the staff he works the closest with at the high school that he'll live forever. He isn't the biggest fan of the jokes because he prefers to stay out of the limelight, not to mention making him existential about his age.
  • Those that have known him long enough know his absolute passion for music. He doesn't really talk about it alot unless you prompt him. Usually incredibly bashful about his musical abilities. He swaps between acoustic & electric guitar & enjoys singing. Sounding like if Hozier & Eddie Vedder had a baby.
  • Though other than doing some shows with some old college friends when he goes to Denver once a year, he doesn't do much other with it other than personal enjoyment & practice. He's pretty open to new music, usually getting a word about it from his students or Erin. He's quite far from a pretrencious person when it comes to music--he'll listen to pretty much anything that jives with him.
  • Don't ask him about his love life, because it's nonexistant. He's had flings & interests earlier in his life, but they've all fizzled out or they've gone on separate paths. He doesn't mind being single, having gotten quite used to living that way. Though Véronica tries to encourage him to do SOMETHING. Though she never has much luck. He thinks he's at the age now where there's not much use in dating. Who would want to date a fossil like him?
