rae's page

learn more about my character, rae!

Rae is one of the side characters im my story, Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought. She is one of Erin's older sisters. Being a fraternal twin with Iris. It might be in your best interest to leave her be...

It's evident Rae has something going on. She prefers to be left alone, either ignoring those that strike up conversation or snapping back at them if they're persistent. She may keep to herself, but she's not afraid to let you know your company is not wanted.

School...doesn't seem like her strong suit. Skipping class entirely, sleeping through class, or she's just off in her own mind--either way, not paying attention. She also gets REALLY angry if you ask her to read aloud in front of people. Being really ashamed about her dyslexia. Though it seems math is the only one she will least TRY to listen & understand. Thomas making sure she gets time with the only teacher that seems to know how to deal with her.

It seems the only real knack she has, & will try in persistently in, is track. Thomas is thankful for it because it keeps her from being kicked out--easily leading their team to state championships. Her track coach is also strict with her, wanting her to stop doing shit just because--which Rae usually butts up against. Though, if she had the choice, she'd listen to music & run all day...

what are they like?

Erin finds it embarrassing, Iris more worried about Rae getting in trouble. Rae can't stand injustice, ESPECIALLY from people with absolutely no backbone.
