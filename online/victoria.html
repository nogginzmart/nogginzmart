victoria's page

learn more about my character, victoria!

Victoria is one of the side characters in my story, Dedicated To The Kidz I Fought. Victoria has just recently moved to Crested Butte, Colorado from New York with her adoptive mother, Gianna, her adoptive, younger brother, Ollie, Ollie's dog, Beav, & her beloved calico cat, Thor. Even though she's...not really interested in the country hick lifestyle, she wants her mum to be happy & feel more spiritually connected, so she agreed to go along with the move.

It's the beginning of the Fall 2008 school year, & Victoria is starting her 9th grade year. She stuck out like a sore thumb going in, but least most of the attention was positive--curious why her family woul move all the way to a small mountain town like Crested Butte. Victoria finds this a great way to insert herself into the preestablished social hierachy.

Because there's no dance team at this new school, she joins cheerleading to fill that dance hole in her heart. Which gets her acquainted with the more popular girls in her class. As a result, they "take her in" as a friend & get her acclimated. It doesn't take long into the school year that others recognize that Victoria is one of them.

She doesn't mind Ollie's new friends, she's happy he's making some so fast, though brushes them off as nothing more than...his friends. He's annoying, so they shouldn't be too far off from him. Their relationship isn't bad, per say, but he can get on her nerves sometimes & she prefers to be left out of his antics.

what are they like?

  • Victoria is an interesting character, being all what you'd expect from a preppy teenage cheerleader, but at the same time she prefers to be left alone with her TV time, internet time, & doesn't like going to more social events such as football games or "parties" (very very sanitized high school parties). Especially if it means her friends are trying to get her to hookup with some random boy they think she'll like.
  • You didn't misread earlier--when Gianna adopted Victoria, she knew she would have the capability to learn witchcraft, just like herself. Victoria joined her mother in her practice by her own accord, so it's a way the two bond with one another. Victoria doesn't talk about it for many reasons, but it's definitely a way she feels different from other kids her age.
  • Thor has always been with Victoria. Like...literally. When she was still in the orphanage, he came into where they all slept & she was still awake. Although he tried to deter her from getting anywhere near him, he was astonished when she could understand the things he was saying. Her finding him quite rude. So, out of curiosity, he stuck around with her. The two working together to keep him hidden so he didn't get thrown out.
  • When Gianna was in the process of adopting Victoria & she showed her Thor, she was quite amazed she had been able to hide a whole cat from the nuns in the orphanage. Though she was charmed by him, though isn't able to understand him like Victoria. So the two have been together ever since, but...Victoria is confused by Thor doesn't age at all. Though she prefers not to question it too much.
  • Ollie & Victoria have a pretty normal sibling relationship. Victoria finds him annoying, Ollie finds her spoiled & conceited, but get over their personal perspectives & jam out to music or fight over playing video games. Gianna doesn't have to intervene often, but after giving "the look" they usually shut up or keep it to just distainful glares.
  • Victoria isn't a slacker when it comes to school. She gets her work done, she holds herself to high standards, & holds to her ambitions. She finds Gianna admirable because she's a single parent with an affluencial job & can provide for the two of them, especially in such dire economic times. Gianna prefers to keep herself humble about it, but appreciates the thought. Victoria stands out more amongst her new friends because of how she views school, usually having to ignore them during class so she can get her work done.
  • When Victoria eventually meets Rae, she's worried someone new will throw off her power as the newer, different girl. Though after seeing more of...what she's like, she feels secure that she won't challenge her social standing. Though grows well aware that Rae isn't anyone to be messed with, though much to her dismay, her friends find much joy in making fun of her & her fraternal twin sister, Iris.
