Erin Haverathyn

they/them

a 13-year-old nobody who would prefer to be left alone. They're the younger sibling to Rae & Iris & Thomas is their guardian. You'll usually find them with a nose buried in a book, with headphones on listening to some music, or mulling over school work. Though they always sport some type of camera, waiting for the perfect shot...

Ollie Coppola

he/they

a 12-year-old boy who just moved to Crested Butte, Colorado from New York with his family, & he's excited to make new friends & experiences! He's the younger, adoptive brother to Victoria & Gianna's biological son. You'd probably see him yapping with someone, skateboarding, or walking his beloved dog, Beav!

Brooke Ignacio

she/her

a 13-year-old girl who loves bugs & a good book! Willow's pseudo sister, Veronica's biological daughter, & a bit of a wallflower. Willow usually dragging her around with her emo friends. You'll usually find her reading, either fiction or research about bugs, or getting through her school work.

Willow Ignacio

she/they

a 14-year-old girl, the oldest kid in their grade, Brooke's pseudo sister, & Iah's biological daughter. A loud, rebellious, & school hating scene girl who's usually seen yapping with her emo friends, threatening bullies, or being not subtle in neglecting her schoolwork.

Thomas Eliphas

he/him

A 50-year-old highschool English teacher. He is Erin, Rae, & Iris's guardian & Veronica's pseudo brother. He's a kind teacher, enjoying his job, & tries his best as a guardian. Though it doesn't seem he has much of a social life outside of work...

Iris Haverathyn

she/her

A 17-year-old girl with an eye & talent for technology & a love for all things nerdy. She's one of Erin's older sisters,fraternal twins with Rae, Thomas being her guardian, & Cecilia is her best friend! You may see her around, but she's often with her chunky headphones on in her own little world.

Rae Haverathyn

she/they

A 17-year-old girl with massive anger issues, preferring to be left alone. She's one of Erin's older sisters & fraternal twins with Iris. It's seldom you'll see her around in class, usually skipping to smoke or avoid classwork. Though that's for the best...it's not advised to get in her way.

Victoria Coppola

she/her

A 16-year-old girl who just moved to Crested Butte, Colorado from New York with her family. Ollie being her younger, adoptive brother, Gianna her adoptive mother, & Thor's owner. Even though she's a popular cheerleader, she's a headstrong & dedicated student--usually finding her friends annoying when they get in the way of her schoolwork.

Thor Coppola

he/it

An...old(?) & devious cat, having been helplessly dragged across the country with his owner's, Victoria, family. He prefers to be left alone to his antics, usually terrorizing Ollie or watching people. Victoria finds him annoying, but enjoys his company nonetheless
