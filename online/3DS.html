3DS Homebrew

learn about my experience with modding my 3DS console!

not to mention links & guides to resources that helped me!

So, YOU want to mod YOUR 3DS/2DS console? or...not & you just wanna see how I did it or check out the page (if that's the case, thank you!). Well, you're in the right place!

As a marginally tech savvy person--I found this process to be fairly easy. Though I definitely had moments I was like "WHAT THE FUCK AM I DOING!?!?", so do take what I offer with a grain of salt!!

Everything I will provide will be what will actually help you mod your console--I will not be giving a step-by-step tutorial! I've only modded 2 consoles, so I don't want to claim to be an expert or anything!!

Furthermore, I am not responsible if you brick your console. Now it is VERY hard with these processes in these guides to brick your console unless you were purposefully trying--but if you do happen to do so, uh...good luck! I hope you backed up your NAND!

Finally, I will try my best to keep things updated, but I highly recommend just sticking to the main guides & refraining from relying on videos. I found them useful as a visual learner, but these programs are being actively updated. These videos can go out of date fast--so please proceed with caution!

why did you mod your console?

After doing extensive research on 3DS modding, why wouldn't I? It adds SO much life to your 3DS & honestly drives me to play with it more. I can bond with other modded 3DS/2DS owners over Streetpass & Miiverse, I can play with people on Mario Kart 7 online in CTGP-7, & I get to finally play games I've never had the chance to touch!
I know it won't be for everyone, but my 3DS is so...genuinely me since I've modded it. I make my own themes, I have all these badges to decorate my home screen & folders, & I'm also not on my phone as much because of all I can do on my 3DS!
If you have the money, knowledge, & time--mod a 3DS/2DS. Plus if you already own another console in the family, breathe life back into it!! I modded my little brother's 2DS & he was ecstatic! I got him all the fanmade 3DS ports for FNAF 1-4 & got him some original DS games he's always wanted to play with TwilightMenu++!
Though if you're going to do it, do it soon, or search a while to find the best deals on a 3DS/2DS! They're getting really expensive due to rising nostalgia & modding capabilities, so get out there!
