learn more about my character, skye!

Skye is one of the side characters in my story, Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought. In the cold winter months of October of 2008, Skye starts living with the Coppola's after the insistance of her mother. Skye is the child of Ollie's other parent--making them half-siblings. Gianna is more than happy to let Skye stay with them, letting Skye take the former guest room as their own bedroom.

Skye takes to their new school fairly well, joining Victoria in her freshman year at Crested Butte Community School. Their gender ambiguity causing them to attract the attention of different ground of their classmates, making Victoria annoyed. Ollie is more than thrilled to have a half sibling--especially a cool & effortlessly nonchalant one like Skye!

Skye tends to keep themsevles, but isn't afraid to chat it up with their classmates when they initiate conversation. Though they DEFINIETLY brush off any romantic interests from their classmates, but they're definitely flattered they're popular & seen as attractive. They even joined basketball after being told they'd probably be good at it. They find it interesting--& it gives them something to do, so what's there to complain about? Though they were...definitely confused on what team to put them on.

Skye is...different, but still quite friendly. Stands out but blends into the crowd at the same time. Victoria thinks something is up about them, but Gianna says there's nothing for her to worry about. Much to her dismay...Gianna never keeps things under wraps with them.

what are they like?

  • Skye is very chill & sociable--kind & pretty adaptable to anyone! They find people fascinating, & even if they ultimately don't agree or get along with them, they find the interaction valuable in some sense. Though this attracts a lot of attention their way, which isn't the most favorable to them. They're flattered people like them, but ultimatly likes sticking to the background.
  • Skye is a frequent daydreamer, usually having to be snapped back to the real world when in class or out & about wandering. Whatever they're thinking about, it captivates their attention.
  • They love having siblings like Ollie & Victoria. Loving to hear what they have to say about their issues or daily hyjinks--big or small.
  • They're more attracted to the mundane everyday rather than anything outright exciting. Though trying new things intrigues them, so it usually works out in the end, but if it's crazy for no reason...they'll be disgruntled about it.
  • Them & Rae are tight, but they don't always agree with Rae's more...irresponsible & reckless decisions. Especially those directed towards Victoria, of which the two have a supposed...rivalry, or whatever. Strong personalities clash, they get that, but it kinda dampens what makes Rae fun to be around--along with Victoria.
  • Though, besides that, the two usually hang out the most. They'll hang out in town after class or on the weekends, in the parking lot after or before class, & usually sit with one another at lunch. Skye doesn't usually partake in Rae's frequent smoke breaks or skipping class, but Rae usually offers anyway.
  • Skye & Iris get along, kind to one another & could talk for hours of allowed. Though with their varying schedules they usually don't cross paths. Skye wishes they could see her more often.
  • Gianna & them spend a lot of time chatting. Although the two aren't biologically related, Skye feels like Gianna was the other mother they never had. Especially with how their own mother would describe her, Skye couldn't be more thankful to be meeting her in the flesh, let alone be allowed to live with her.
