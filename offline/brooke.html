brooke's page

learn more about my character, brooke!

Brooke is one of the main characters in my story, Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought. As the Fall 2008 school comes around once more, Brooke is prepared for another, standard school year. Merely happy to have access to the school library again & getting outside the house.

Though when she enters her class, there's a murmur of a new kid joining their class. Ollie Coppola waltzed into their class & he's immediately the center of attention. Although Brooke thought he was sweet, she kept her distance. Prefering to stay out of the spotlight.

Though as the first week goes by, Ollie--dragging Erin along--introduces himself to Brooke & tries to get to know her. She's more than happy to talk to him & Erin, & tells Willow about it later--never really having the best experience making friends. Though Willow is initially apprehensive, & although Brooke tries to ease her worries. Willow has never been the type of person to listen.

Much to Brooke's horror, Willow almost goes through with beating the shit out of Ollie--so Erin & Brooke intervene before Ollie got grinded into mush. & after Ollie talks to Willow for longer than a second, Willow is totally won over by him & excited someone wants to be friends with Brooke. Brooke is...not surprised, but incredibly apologetic.

& as Willow pushes herself in their little group, Brooke is thrown into her first friends group where she wasn't just...inserted in. Not to mention rekindling the familial & friendly relationship with Erin, Brooke feels...a little off about such a drastic change to her everyday normal, but maybe this will be a good change.

what are they like?

  • Brooke is quiet & reserved but kind. Even though she doesn't put herself out there as the most sociable person, she tries to be kind to everyone she meets. Unless they give her a reason to act otherwise, & even then she just ignores them or gives them the cold shoulder.
  • She's not odd, persay, like Willow--oh whom has more niche & loud interests. She considers herself quite normal! ...Minus her obsession with bugs. She just finds them cute!! People give them such a bad rep! not to mention they're INCREDIBLY fascinating!! Her mother isn't the biggest fan of her obsession either, though encourages her to pursue it anyways. ...even if it makes her skin crawl.
  • Though other than bugs, she's considers herself pretty normal!! Willow tries to roap her into her shenanigans, & Brooke is definitely interested in the things she does, but usually doesn't pursue them on her own time.
  • She's kind of a romantic, usually sitting with her mom when she watches cheesy telenovellas. She hopes to find her person one day, but her self esteem usually gets in her way. Not to mention she doesn't feel conventionally, or any type of, attractive.
  • Brooke has a complicated relationship with her mother, Véronica. She loves her immensely, but they definiely butt heads sometimes. Usually because Veronica is reactive & stubborn while Brooke tends to be passive. Plus when Willow does something that sets Veronica off & ends up taking it out a bit on Brooke. Plus their family is...complicated with her Dad being off at war. Plus with how finances have been...their relationship has been quite strained as a result.
  • Since they've been living with them for so long, Brooke considers Iah another mother. She relies on her a lot for more emotional needs, like comfort or someone to vent to. Iah usually being more than happy to lend an ear, even if she's confused by a lot of cultural things. Though Brooke appreciates how much she tries.
  • Brooke struggles a lot with her weight. Not that she eats unhealthily, she actually doesn't eat enough half the time, but compared to her classmates & what popular culture deems as "healthy"...she feels like the odd one out. Struggling a lot in finding fashionable & appropriate clothes for her. Thankfully, Willow, Veronica,& Iah band together to help find her cuter clothes that accomodate her. Brooke tries her best to style her clothes in a way where she still feels cute, but not feeling like some "freak" for what she looks like.
  • Due to a lot of the aforementioned factors, & being considered "poor" compared to her classmates, Brooke doesn't have much luck finding friends. A lot of them considering her a "wallflower". So Willow usually drags her along with her to hang out with her & her emo friends. Brooke feels like the odd one out, & knows she's not necessarily welcome nor included with the others in the group, but heaven be damned if they don't do what Willow says. Brooke appreciates the thought, nonetheless.
  • Brooke is a good student, & thoroughly enjoys the learning aspect of school! Taking a part in the book club & plays clarinet in concert band. She tries to maximize her time there as possible. She's in advanced placement, along with Erin. Though since Willow is in the normal classes she tends to be lonely most of the school day. Veronica & Iah can't really help with their schoolwork, so Willow usually relies on Brooke & Brooke tries her best to figure it out herself.
  • She wouldn't hate winter is she didn't freeze up! Due to her snakes, her body gets rigid & unable to move after a certain temperature (even if her snakes aren't visible). So she usually has to bundle up HEAVILY or stay inside. It makes going to class during the winter & late fall quite difficult.
  • Furthermore about her snakes, most don't know about them. it's just immediate family, because Véronica has snakes as well, & Iah isn't bothered by them. Willow finds them cool because she thinks Brooke is a mythical creature or something. Brooke loves them, having names for each of her snakes. Thomas & Erin are aware of the snakes as will, neither of them particularly bothered by them.
