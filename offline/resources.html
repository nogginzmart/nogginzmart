welcome to the resources page!

want to make your own Neocities site?

see what stuff I used to make mine!!

  • Tumblr - blinkies, web stamps, favicons, & dividers
  • Pinterest - backgrounds & headers
  • W3Schools - CSS & HTML help & lessons to learn how to code!
  • ImageColorPicker.com - hex codes for CSS!
  • Google & Reddit - when it doubt about coding? search solutions! ( NO AI!! LOOK AT THE SOURCES!! TEST IT OUT!! )
  • Fluffmoth's identity buttons - pretty self explanatory--lots of options & I use them a lot for my about me & OC pages!
  • ID button maker - in the same style of the buttons in the entry before, you can make custom ones!! Which I used here & there for the OC pages & for the art gallery navigation buttons!!
  • ribo.zone - I specifically used their cyanobacteria template for several sites (even this one!)
  • chattable - the site where I made the chatbox on my front page (CURRENTLY NOT ACCEPTING NEW ACCOUNTS! SORRY!)
  • NomnomNami - the person who made the template I used for my blog!! They even answered an email I sent with questions I had! Very nice & amazing template!
  • Rarebit - the person who made the comic template I am using!! complicated but incredibly fascinating when you get it working!
  • CRT Effect Tutorial - self explanatory, was really helpful! please check them out!
  • audio player - the template I used for the audio players at the top of my pages!
  • webringu - webring template & help from Petrapixel!
  • front page quiz help - the guide I used to help me make the front page quiz!
