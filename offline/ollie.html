ollie's page

learn more about my character, ollie!

Ollie is one of the main characters in my story, Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought. Ollie has just recently moved into Crested Butte, Colorado from New York with his mum, Gianna, his older, adopted sister Victoria, his dog, Beav, & Victoria's evil calico cat, Thor. Although he's was initially apprehensive to make such a big change, he takes this change as an opportunity to make new friends & meet all the new people in his new school & town!

It's the beginning of the Fall 2008 school year, & Ollie is starting the 7th grade at Crested Butte Community School. He's greeted with lots of curiosity, confused why someone would move all the way to this quiet, mountain town. Ollie being more than thrilled to describe his life back in New York & what it was like to all his new classmates.

Though he notices another kid in his class who seems to keep to themselves, Erin. Determined to try & make a good impression to everyone, he approaches them. After getting to know them a little more, & intrigued by their voluntary reclusion, insists to hang around & provide them with some well needed company--much to their dismay.

Although he gets along with pretty much anybody, he seems to prefer sticking with those who either prefer being on their own or struggle to find their own crowd. So with Erin & eventually Willow & Brooke, he feels like he's found his crowd. Them reminding him of the friends he had back home. Plus it helps his mom approves of them.

what are they like?

  • Ollie tries to least be surface level friends with everyone!! Even at his old, & much larger school--he seemed to get along with anyone that crossed paths with him. Due to enjoying that energy back home, he wants to have a similar relationship with his classmates at his new school!! Which goes over fairly well, especially since people are fascinated that he came from so far away.
  • He is 100% a momma's boy--him not being afraid to wear an outfit she picked out for him, a scarf she knitted, a shirt she made-- helps she's pretty observant when it comes to things he likes! He also doesn't "rebel" much, is his mom has a sneaking suspicion he's doing something wrong, he usually cracks under the pressure & confesses to her.
  • His relationship with his older sister, Victoria, is pretty standards for an older sister younger brother dynamtic. They routinely terrorize one another & butt heads sometimes. Gianna usually shuts it all down pretty quick, but she's aware there's no entirely stopping it. Ollie doesn't dislike Victoria, not at all, but finds some of things she does as obnoxious or disruptive. Not to mention thinks she's full of herself sometimes.
  • Ollie LOVES his dog, Beav!!!! He's usually the one to take her out on walks, usually exploring town. Beav usually sleeps in his bed at night, & if Ollie could take her to school with him--he would.
  • He struggles being perpetually seen as the younger brother friend. Is it his maturity? his interets? No one ever seems to explain it much other than that's just how they see him. He tries to get his way out of it, but not to much success.
