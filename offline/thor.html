Thor is...a cat, in my story Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought. He has been dragged along by his owner, Victoria & her family from New York to the quiet, mountain town, Crested Butte, Colorado. He doesn't really care where life takes him, but he would've preferred to not be cooped up in a cage like some beast.

Only Victoria can hear him, & he always has a lot to say. Usually...mean things about others, being a hateful little creature, but he's genuienly curious about the humans around him & why they do what they do. Though after living with Victoria for so long, there isn't much that surprises him anymore.
