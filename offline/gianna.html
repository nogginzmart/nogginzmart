gianna's page

learn more about my character, gianna!

Gianna is one of the side characters in my story "Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought". Gianna has recently moved from New York to Crested Butte, Colorado with her kids Ollie, Victoria, & their pets Thor & Beav. After getting a work opportunity at a hospital in Gunnison, & consulting whether the kids would be up to such a large change, they packed up their bags & headed west! & as the Fall 2008 semester rolls around at the local school, it'll be Ollie & Victoria's first days in a brand new school. Gianna being all the excited for the two of them!

Although she's lived in New York for most of her life, she's optimistic & excited for all she can do out here in Colorado. Though she couldn't have felt more lucky to have sold their townhouse & have their house built when they did. It being a lot more cheap than she expected due to the crash. They all definitely got some stares moving into town with their brand new house in the middle of the crisis.

She never had many friends back in New York, so starting fresh wasn't too bad. Especially since she had cut her father entirely out of her life. So now it's just her & her kids, & she couldn't he happier. Though she's hopeful about meeting people in this new town. Wanting to get involved in the local farmers' market with her new chicken's eggs & produce she'll grow in her blossoming garden & greenhouse.

what are they like?

  • Considering how resourceful she is, you'd think she can from a background where they had to make everything last. Quite the opposite, actually--her father was quite well off & gave Gianna quite a comfortable youth. Putting her though a pretigious education & her physical needs were met, though with the small amount of time she knew her mother she always wanted to be like her. Resourceful, appreciative of the things in her life, & a humble green witch. So even though Gianna went through the motions of being successful in the normal way, she finds for mileage in living life in the way she knew her mother would've wanted her to.
  • She initially adopted Victoria because she didn't think she'd ever have children of her own. Plus with her introverted nature she didn't think she'd have much luck finding someone to love, not understanding at that point she had little interest in men. Even though she eventually had Ollie, her adoration of Victoria never changed. Gianna fostering her into a capable young witch, her still trying to find which type of magic to specialize in.
  • Although Gunnison is a bit of a commute for work, Gianna thought Crested Butte had the better school--not to mention a more inviting town environment. Ultimately deciding that she'd rather commute if it meant her kids would have an easier time getting adjusted & live living where they are.
  • Most of the time she'll have snacks prepared for Ollie & Victoria to have after school. Them having an allotted time in the afternoon to get homework & assignments done. Then they'd sit together at dinner & discuss their days. She looks forward to it everyday, & she hates when she has to miss it because of a medical emergency or a particularly long shift.
  • When Erin & Ollie becomes friends, she's flattered that their house becomes the usual hang out spot. Finding Erin reserved but smart & respectful kid. & when Brooke & Willow come along she loves them much the same. Though she definitely has to remind them to lower their voices when they get particularly tilted at Mario Kart Wii
  • She hopes she'll get the courage to talk to the other parents in the community more. It would be nice to have some friends for a change. Especially since she finds the parents of Ollie's friends quite kind.
