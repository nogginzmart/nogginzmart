iPod Life

tired of spotify, apple music, or youtube music ruining your joy of listening to music on the go?

want a SIGNIFICANTLY cheaper way to listen to music & actually own the music you listen to?

well, let me show you how to enter & navigate the life of having an iPod!!

Now, I'm not going to lie & say it isn't a big change. It's definitely something to get used to. A lot of the stuff we use nowadays have the perk of being convenient, & a lot of people didn't grow up without a smartphone. So there's no shame in thinking it's not for you! Remember, it may be more convenient, but you will own the music you listen to, you don't have to pay for it, & you're not dealing with a soulless corporation who funds harmful things & doesn't pay the artists! Plus I also gradually weened away from using Spotify & when I finally cancelled my subscription it felt like cutting the cord!

Furthermore, when I talk about cheaper, in the LONG TERM it is. Especially if you get a refurbished device from the site I bought mine, they're made with new items inside plus lots more storage--so their price reflects that. Not to mention add-ons that you'll need depending on what you're looking for with listening to music on the go. But your wallet will definitely be thanking you in the long run!!

What Do You Use?

In the right column, you can see a picture of my iPod! Now this isn't just ANY iPod--it's a refurbished model!! Now you don't have to get one like I did--there are listings for different models on Amazon, Ebay--just look for it!! Though I highly recommend it because you can get them with more storage, bluetooth capabilities, & a new battery. Not to mention cool cosmetics, like mine!!

  • my iPod! - this is the EXACT listing & seller of the iPod I own!! This is also the site I got my bluetooth adaptor from!!
  • Spotify Music Downloader - one of the sites I use to acquire my music!
  • Spotify Downloader - this site is good for when you have larger playlists & want a swifter way to download them from Spotify!
  • Youtube to MP3 Converter - this is helpful because some songs won't download over Spotify Music Downloader, so this is a great second option in case! Not to mention you can download some songs that aren't on Spotify!
  • also you'll NEED TO HAVE some form of wired headphones/earbuds, bluetooth adaptor for bluetooth headphones/earbuds, or an aux cord that fit both your headphones & iPod. You CANNOT listen to the music on your iPod without them!
  • You will also need an iTunes account! This is where you'll be hosting all your music & where you'll transfer ALL your music to your iPod!! you can also tranfer music from CD's to iTunes & put them on your iPod if you would prefer to do it like that!!

That's pretty much it for necessary things!! Again, the iPod experience is really customizable & you can choose however you wish to go about it!! Plus you get such euphoria from doing it--I enjoy using my iPod so much & it beats having Spotify sucking money out of my wallet just the give it to dipshits who wanna ruin our world.

now get out there & get yourself an iPod! Plus there's always guides on the internet if you're more curious on learning more about living with an iPod!! Plus I'm always open to discuss how I use it, so if you guys are interested, just let me know!!
