erin's page

learn more about my character, erin!

Erin is one of the main characters in my story "Dedicated to The Kidz I Fought". As the Fall 2008 school year comes back around, they're wanting to make a drastic change to what it means to be "them". Who needs friends? People to talk to? Books, music, & getting work done, & going back home is just much more appealing.

But there's a new family in town--the Coppola's--coming all the way from New York! The youngest, Ollie Coppola, is in Erin's class! He's keen on getting to know everyone, even the loner Erin--MUCH to their dismay.

More about Erin, people are not their niche. Although they've tried to make friends, in their own way. It either falls flat & people are turned off by their efforts, or they're interested in the wrong people--driving Erin to feel discouraged. As a result, they prefer to stick to what they're good at, which is their studies, reading, photography, or just listening to music.

They're not entirely alone, however, having a guardain who cares a lot about them, Thomas. Although they appreciate it & know he wants them to make friends, they internalize it & think it's something innate about them that's inherently unlikeable.

Even though they've known Brooke & Willow for a long while, it's not till Ollie is adament on getting to know Brooke that the three actually start bonding. Even if it's...as a result of Willow almost pummeling Ollie into paste.

what are they like?

  • Erin is pretty...straight forward. They keep to themselves, & tend to come off hostile & aloof. Not to mention a little pretentious, finding more "main stream" interests shallow (it's definitely projection). It gives some reason as to WHY people don't want to hang out with them. They weren't always this way, but felt they might as well unmask if no one is going to like them pretending.
  • This isn't to excuse how they treat others--they're still young & not used to unmasking. Not to mention not really...knowing much about themselves yet either. They're in those years where you start exploring that. Explaining their DRASTIC aesthetic & attitude change.
  • Erin can conversate normally, but it always tends to come off as stilted, monotone, & stiff. A lot of that is unintentional, however. Thomas is used to it & Erin usually shows more emotion if they're irritated or showing sass--of which he DEFINITELY picks up on. Usually getting onto them for it.
  • Thomas & Erin are close. However they don't really...explain to others why they call him Thomas & not dad or anything else, really. To them it just isn't that complicated, & it really isn't. They spend a lot of time together, especially when there's down time at the house. Usually sitting together to watch evening TV like Jeopardy & Wheel of Fortune--taking turns answering things & trying to figure stuff out.
  • School comes easy to Erin--enjoying the mental stimulation. They'd prefer to work on it all on their own rather than paying attention to lecture. Usually finishing early to bury their nose back in a book, much to some of their teacher's dismay. Thomas usually having to come vouch for them.
  • Erin has a couple of special interests--those including music, the internet, & books. Erin plows through books pretty fast--usually taking them a week or two to get through a pretty dense text. It's apparent their fascination with music--collecting CD's, following forums online, burning CD's of their favorite songs to different favorite artists, & they write some "songs" themselves! Though they don't think they're any good.
  • Furthermore, they play alto sax in the concert band! Thomas suggested it since they enjoy music so much, & learning it might make them feel closer to it. They've done it long enough they don't see much of a reason to get away from it. Plus they do a lot of interesting pieces, & they like the energy of the antics that go on in class. Even if they don't directly participate in the antics.
  • It's not like Erin disliked Brooke or Willow before Ollie brought them all together. Erin got introduced to Brooke & Willow at a very tumultuous time in their life. Half the time they wouldn't even speak. The three got along when Erin would have to stay put there at their house or they'd be dropped off at their's, but not much of a relationship other than the given one ever formed. Usually out of anxiety out of Erin & Brooke & Willow just...well she's Willow.
